# Letter push and three distinct skills

User requested a physical, sharper question transition: the question should emerge from a letter and push the existing composition right rather than fade in. They also requested stronger manipulator copy, a larger “Вместе мы научимся” lead-in, three numbered beats rather than four, and varied animation.

The map and its header now move offscreen right as an oversized outline А sweeps across the viewport. Question lines emerge from left-hand clipping windows in sequence. The letter uses an outline so the incoming text remains readable during the transition. Text stays in the accessibility tree; decorative typography is hidden from assistive technology. Reverse scrolling restores the map; reduced motion and no-JS retain the static reading order.

The manipulator text is a blue callout with a yellow edge and enlarged bold actions. “Вместе мы научимся” is the prominent skills heading. The skills are now 0.1 Распознать, 0.2 Дистанцироваться, 0.3 Вернуть себе контроль и нейтрализовать давление. Recognition uses a scanning reveal, distancing moves the word and opens the frame, and control assembles two separated text lines before revealing the final action. The numbered scene lasts 950 px of desktop scroll or 750 px on mobile.
